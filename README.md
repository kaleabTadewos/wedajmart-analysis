# WedajMart: Product Review & Business Analysis

An outside review of the WedajMart prototype, plus research on the Ethiopian e-commerce market, payment rules, and a recommended path forward.

Prepared November 2026. Research uses sources dated 2020–2026; each file cites its sources inline.

## The short version

1. **The prototype is impressive but unfocused.** It looks like a real store, but it tries to be about 20 businesses at once (marketplace, used goods, social-account escrow, video, music, fonts, academy, ads, equb, group buying). All of it runs on demo data.
2. **The market has moved.** Ethio Telecom launched **Zemen Gebeya** inside telebirr in May 2025. It holds payment until delivery and reaches tens of millions of telebirr users. A general "escrow marketplace" now competes head-on with the national telecom.
3. **Payments are no longer the hard part; trust and delivery are.** Telebirr (about 57M users) and gateways like Chapa are widely used. Experts say fulfilment, receipts and dispute handling are where e-commerce in Ethiopia breaks.
4. **Don't hold customer money yourself.** Holding funds is a National Bank of Ethiopia licensed activity (minimum ETB 50M capital for payment instrument issuers). Use a licensed gateway's split-payment features instead.
5. **Pick one narrow wedge that fits a part-time founder.** My recommendation is a **diaspora gifts & Habesha clothing** business. The partner abroad brings diaspora buyers and tech; the partner in Addis manages artisans and delivery. A **seller-tools product for Telegram merchants** is the strongest alternative.
