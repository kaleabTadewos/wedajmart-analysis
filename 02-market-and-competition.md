# 02 · Market & Competition

**Main finding:** In Ethiopia, online payment is mostly solved. Delivery, trust and dispute handling are not. The biggest new competitor, Ethio Telecom's **Zemen Gebeya**, already offers the "payment held until delivery" promise inside telebirr. WedajMart should not fight it head-on as a general marketplace.

## What experts say is broken

From a May 2026 Addis Ababa University symposium ([EBR](https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/?p=17563)):

- **Logistics:** last-mile delivery, weak address system, high cost, no clear rules for returns.
- **Trust:** fake merchants and fraud, weak dispute resolution, no standard digital receipts or refund policies.
- **Cash habits and digital literacy** remain strong barriers.
- **Platform failures:** Jumia, Balesuq and Qefira are cited as platforms that failed, due to regulatory uncertainty, logistics costs and unsustainable pricing.
- EthSwitch's view, paraphrased: the question has shifted from "can people pay online?" to "can we deliver, prove, and support the purchase reliably?"

## Where the openings are

1. **Diaspora demand for Ethiopian goods and gifts.** Buyers abroad pay in hard currency and care about quality and reliability more than price. Today they rely on relatives and informal sellers. Zemen Gebeya is built for local telebirr users, not diaspora card payers.
2. **Tools for Telegram/TikTok sellers.** Thousands of informal sellers already have buyers but no order management, payment links, receipts or delivery booking. Selling to them avoids competing with them.
3. **Verified used electronics.** Fraud fear is real, but this needs a physical inspection point and full-time staff.
4. **Curated niches** (specialty coffee subscriptions, wedding attire, religious crafts), where a generic marketplace looks weak.

## Caveats

- Some sources are 2022–2024 news; numbers for Zmall, beU and Jiji may be outdated.
- TikTok's availability and the status of social media restrictions in Ethiopia have changed over time; check before relying on it as a channel.
- No reliable data exists on Telegram commerce volume.
