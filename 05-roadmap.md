# 05 · Roadmap

A plan for **Option A (diaspora gifts & Habesha goods)** run by a founder working part-time in Addis and a partner abroad. Each phase ends with a **gate**: a result that must be true before spending more time or money. If a gate is missed, fix the phase or rethink; don't add features.

## Who does what

| Area | Partner in Addis | Partner abroad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sellers & sourcing | Finds and visits artisans, negotiates prices, checks quality | — |
| Fulfilment | Packing, courier handover, delivery photos, returns | — |
| Product & tech | Feedback from the ground | Builds and runs the site, payments integration, analytics |
| Marketing & sales | Telegram/TikTok content in Amharic | Diaspora outreach: churches, community groups, Facebook groups, WhatsApp |
| Money & legal | Business registration, licence, TIN, local bank | Initial funding, gateway setup for card payments |

**Put the partnership in writing early:** roles, hours per week, who owns what share, and what happens if one person leaves (vesting over 3–4 years is common). It protects the friendship.

## Phases

| Phase | Time | What gets done | Gate to pass |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0. Focus & clean up** | 2–4 weeks | Cut the prototype to gifts + Habesha goods. Fix routing, search, language toggle and photos. Remove fake data, social-account escrow, equb. Write terms and privacy pages. | 5 people outside the team complete a test order on their phones without help |
| **1. Holiday pilot** | 1 season (e.g. Genna/Timket) | 15–30 curated items from 5–10 artisans. Take orders through the site **and** a Telegram/WhatsApp channel. Card payment via a licensed gateway (or payment links); deliver in Addis with a courier partner and handover photos. | **50 paid orders**, under 5% problems (late, wrong, damaged), at least 10 buyers saying they'd order again |
| **2. Make it a business** | 3–6 months | Register and license properly. Real reviews, order tracking, delivery SLAs. Add non-holiday products (coffee subscriptions, birthdays, weddings). Move customer data to Ethiopian hosting. | **150+ orders/month**, positive profit per order after delivery and ads, **20%+ repeat buyers** |
| **3. Grow or go full-time** | 6–12 months | Ship kemis and coffee abroad; corporate/association gift orders; consider Option B tools for your artisans. | Monthly profit covering a meaningful share of the founder's salary for **3 months in a row** → decision on going full-time |

## Rough budget for phases 0–1

These are **planning estimates**, not quotes; check local prices.

| Item | Estimate |
| --- | --- |
| Business registration, licence, lawyer hour | ~$150–400 |
| Product samples, photography, packaging | ~$300–700 |
| Hosting, domain, gateway setup | ~$20–60 / month |
| Diaspora ads and community outreach for one season | ~$300–800 |
| Courier costs | Charged to customers in the delivery fee |
| **Total to first gate** | **~$1,000–2,500** |

Spend in steps: release the next tranche only when the previous gate is met.

## Numbers to track weekly

- Orders and revenue
- Profit per order after product, packaging, delivery, payment fees and ads
- Repeat buyers (%)
- Delivery problems (%) and refund cost
- Cost to acquire a customer (ad spend ÷ new buyers)
- Seller earnings and seller retention

## When to stop or change course

- After two holiday seasons, buyers don't come back → the promise isn't strong enough; revisit Option B.
- Delivery problems stay above 10% → fix the courier setup before marketing more.
- The founder can't keep up part-time → keep scope smaller, pre-orders only, until revenue pays for help.
