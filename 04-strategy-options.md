# 04 · Strategy Options

**Recommendation:** Start with **Option A, diaspora gifts & Habesha goods**. It fits a founder working part-time in Addis with a partner abroad, earns foreign currency, avoids the telebirr/Zemen Gebeya fight, and reuses most of the current product pages. Keep **Option B, Telegram seller tools** as the software play if the team would rather build a product than run operations.

## The constraints that shape the choice

- The founder in Addis has another job, so the business must run on **evenings and weekends** at first.
- The partner abroad can contribute **tech and some money**, and lives among the target diaspora buyers.
- A general escrow marketplace now competes with **Zemen Gebeya** (Ethio Telecom) and the free reach of **Telegram/TikTok** sellers; see [02-market-and-competition.md](02-market-and-competition.md).
- Holding money needs an NBE licence the team can't get; see [03-payments-and-legal.md](03-payments-and-legal.md).

## Option A in more detail

**Start with gifts to family in Ethiopia, not exports.** Delivering inside Addis avoids customs and international shipping while the team learns.

- **Products (first season):** holiday packages for Genna (Jan 7), Timket (Jan 19) and Fasika, plus coffee ceremony sets, specialty coffee, and kemis/netela to order. Curate 15–30 items, not hundreds.
- **How it works:** a buyer abroad pays by card through a licensed gateway. The Addis partner sources from 5–10 artisans and shops, packs, and delivers via a courier partner (beU/Zmall-type fleets) with a photo or video of the handover.
- **Why it can win:** global gift sites sell generic hampers; local senders are informal and deposit-based. A trusted, Ethiopian-made, photo-confirmed delivery is a clear promise.
- **Phase 2:** ship kemis, coffee and crafts abroad (DHL / Ethiopian Airlines cargo), with a size guide and made-to-measure options.
- **What to cut from the prototype:** used goods, social-account escrow, video, beats, fonts, academy, ads, equb, group pools, live shopping.

## Option B in more detail

- **Product:** a Telegram bot + simple web catalogue per seller; payment links via a licensed gateway; order statuses; Amharic receipts; one-click courier booking.
- **Customer:** fashion, cosmetics and electronics sellers already running Telegram channels with 1,000+ members.
- **Pricing to test:** a free tier, then a small monthly fee or a per-order fee. Willingness to pay is unproven, so validate with 20 seller interviews before building.
- **Advantage:** scales without inventory or couriers; the partner abroad can build most of it.
- **Combination:** Option A's artisans become Option B's first users.

## What "success" should mean in year one

Not a big platform. A business with **repeat customers, positive margin per order, and a clear reason** for the founder to go full-time: for example, monthly profit that covers a meaningful part of his salary for three months in a row. See the gates in [05-roadmap.md](05-roadmap.md).
